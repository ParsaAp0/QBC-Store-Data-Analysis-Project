## Overview
We analyze a retail store's transactional data to uncover meaningful business insights. Our workflow encompasses data cleaning, data warehousing, statistical hypothesis testing, machine learning prediction, and the creation of an interactive executive dashboard. The end goal is to provide the store management with clear, data-driven recommendations to optimize sales, logistics, and profitability.

## Phase 1: Data Preparation and Data Warehousing
We begin by ingesting the raw retail dataset into a structured database. The data undergoes extensive preprocessing and cleaning to handle missing values, inconsistencies, and formatting issues. We then design a Data Warehouse following a Star Schema architecture, which includes clearly defined Fact and Dimension tables. Proper primary and foreign keys are established to maintain relational integrity. All tables and their relationships are then loaded into Power BI to verify that the data model functions correctly for reporting and analysis.

## Phase 2: Statistical Analysis - Impact of Discounts
A central hypothesis in retail is whether offering discounts effectively drives higher sales volumes. To investigate this, we split the dataset into two distinct groups: transactions with discounts and transactions without discounts. We analyze and compare the distribution of sold quantities between these two groups. Using rigorous statistical methods, we determine whether any observed differences in sales volume are statistically significant, thereby confirming or refuting the practical effectiveness of discount strategies.

## Phase 3: Predictive Machine Learning Models
We develop two core predictive models to assist with strategic planning.

1.  **Profit Prediction (Regression)**: We build a regression model that predicts the profit generated by a sold product based on its various features. Careful feature selection and preprocessing are applied to ensure the model is generalizable and does not suffer from overfitting, providing reliable profit estimates for future sales.

2.  **Ship Mode Classification (Multiclass Classification)**: We create a classification model that predicts the shipping method (e.g., Standard, Second Class, First Class) for a given order. This prediction empowers the company to offer targeted, minor discounts to encourage customers to upgrade to higher-tier, more profitable shipping services.

## Phase 4: Business Dashboard and Executive Reporting
All processed data, statistical findings, and machine learning results are integrated into a comprehensive Power BI dashboard tailored for store managers. This dashboard provides intuitive visualizations and answers critical business questions, including:

- Market volume analysis based on average and total sales, identifying which markets are the most viable for investment.
- The relationship between order amount and shipping costs, revealing whether high-value orders correlate with higher shipping expenditures.
- Average shipping times broken down by shipping mode and individual countries, enabling region-specific logistics oversight.
- Sales performance distribution across the days of the week to pinpoint peak shopping days.
- Profitability analysis at both the product category level and the individual product level to identify the most lucrative inventory items.
- A custom-defined metric that measures the deviation of each product's profit from the overall average, used to determine which geographic regions contribute the most significant profit margins.

Additionally, we incorporate   creative reports to the dashboard, providing even deeper insights and a more comprehensive overview of the business operations.

## Key Outcomes and Deliverables
Upon completion, this project delivers a fully functional end-to-end data analysis solution. The final outputs include a clean, relational data warehouse, validated statistical evidence on discount effectiveness, operational machine learning models, and an interactive Power BI dashboard. Together, these deliverables equip store management with the necessary tools to make informed, strategic decisions regarding pricing, marketing, logistics, and inventory management.
